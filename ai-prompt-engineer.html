🧠 Prompt Engineering Tips

01Start with the subject → add style → add technical specs — order matters.
02Use --ar in Midjourney for aspect ratio. Sora/Kling prefer natural language descriptions.
03In Runway, describe camera movement first — it heavily influences scene composition.
04Use weighted terms in Midjourney: cinematic lighting::2 gives it more importance.
05Negative prompts in Midjourney use --no text, watermarks. In Runway, write "avoid...".
06For Sora, describe the full timeline — beginning, middle, end — for best video coherence.
